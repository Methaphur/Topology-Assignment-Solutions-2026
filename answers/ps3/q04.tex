Let
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\tau_1$ denote the product topology on $Y$,
  \item $\tau_2$ denote the subspace topology on $Y$ inherited from $X$.
\end{itemize}
We shall prove $\tau_1=\tau_2$.

Let $B$ be a basic open set in $X$. Then
\[ B=\bigcap_{\alpha\in F}\pi_\alpha^{-1}(U_\alpha), \]
where each $U_\alpha$ is open in $X_\alpha$. By the definition of the subspace topology, a
basic open set in $Y$ is $B\cap Y$. Now,
\begin{align*}
  B\cap Y &= Y\cap\bigcap_{\alpha\in F}\pi_\alpha^{-1}(U_\alpha)\\
          &= \bigcap_{\alpha\in F}\bigl(Y\cap\pi_\alpha^{-1}(U_\alpha)\bigr)\\
          &= \bigcap_{\alpha\in F}q_\alpha^{-1}(U_\alpha\cap Y_\alpha).
\end{align*}
Since $U_\alpha$ is open in $X_\alpha$, the set $U_\alpha\cap Y_\alpha$ is open in $Y_\alpha$ by
the subspace topology. Therefore, $B\cap Y$ is open in the product topology on $Y$. Hence
$\underline{\tau_2\subseteq\tau_1}$.

Let $C$ be a basic open set in the product topology on $Y$. Then
\[ C=\bigcap_{\alpha\in F}q_\alpha^{-1}(V_\alpha), \]
where $V_\alpha$ is open in $Y_\alpha$. Since $Y_\alpha$ has the subspace topology inherited
from $X_\alpha$, $\exists$ an open set $U_\alpha$ in $X_\alpha$ such that
$V_\alpha=U_\alpha\cap Y_\alpha$. Therefore,
\begin{align*}
  C &= \bigcap_{\alpha\in F}q_\alpha^{-1}(U_\alpha\cap Y_\alpha)\\
    &= Y\cap\bigcap_{\alpha\in F}\pi_\alpha^{-1}(U_\alpha).
\end{align*}
But $\bigcap_{\alpha\in F}\pi_\alpha^{-1}(U_\alpha)$ is open in $X$ because it is a basic open
set in the product topology on $X$. Thus, $C$ is the intersection of $Y$ with an open set in
$X$. Hence $C$ is open in the subspace topology on $Y$. Therefore
$\underline{\tau_1\subseteq\tau_2}$.

So, $\underline{\tau_1=\tau_2}$.
