\begin{proof}
We are given that $p\colon X\to Y$ is an open map and that $A$ is an open subset of $X$.
Consider the restriction of $p$ to $A$, defined by
\[ q\colon A\longrightarrow p(A),\qquad q(x)=p(x),\quad x\in A. \]
We need to show that $q$ is open.

Note that a map is open if the image of every open subset of its domain is open in its
codomain.

Let $U$ be an arbitrary open subset of $A$. Since $U$ is open in the subspace topology on
$A$, there exists an open subset $V$ of $X$ such that
\[ U=A\cap V. \]
Now, both $A$ and $V$ are open in $X$. Therefore, their intersection is also open in $X$.
Hence, $U$ is open in $X$.

Since $p\colon X\to Y$ is an open map, the image of every open subset of $X$ is open in $Y$.
Consequently, $p(U)$ is open in $Y$.

On the other hand, since $U\subseteq A$, we have
\[ p(U)\subseteq p(A). \]
Also, by the definition of the restriction $q$, we obtain
\[ q(U)=p(U). \]
Thus, $q(U)$ is an open subset of $Y$ contained in $p(A)$. By the definition of the subspace
topology, $q(U)$ is therefore open in $p(A)$.

Since $U$ was an arbitrary open subset of $A$, the image of every open subset of $A$ under $q$
is open in $p(A)$. Therefore,
\[ q\colon A\to p(A)\ \text{is an open map.} \qedhere \]
\end{proof}
