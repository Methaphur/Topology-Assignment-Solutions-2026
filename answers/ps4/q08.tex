\textbf{Proof.}

The lower limit topology has the basis
\[ \mathcal{B}=\{[a,b):a<b\}. \]

Consider
\[ U=(-\infty,0),\qquad V=[0,\infty). \]

We show that both sets are open.

If $x\in U$, then
\[ x\in[x,0)\subseteq U. \]
Since $[x,0)$ is a basis element, $U$ is open.

Similarly, if $x\in V$, then
\[ x\in[x,x+1)\subseteq V. \]
Thus, $V$ is also open.

Moreover,
\[ U\cap V=\emptyset,\qquad U\cup V=\R. \]

Both sets are non-empty. Therefore, $(U,V)$ forms a separation of $\R_{\ell}$.

Hence,
\[ \boxed{\R_{\ell}\text{ is not connected.}} \]
\hfill $\square$
