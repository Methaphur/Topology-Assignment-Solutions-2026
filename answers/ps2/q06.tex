Let $S$ be the collection of sets of the form
\[
  (a,\infty)=\{x\in X:a<x\}
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  (-\infty,b)=\{x\in X:x<b\}.
\]

For two rays of the same type, we have
\[
  (a,\infty)\cap(c,\infty)=(\max\{a,c\},\infty)
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  (-\infty,b)\cap(-\infty,d)=(-\infty,\min\{b,d\}).
\]
Also, for two rays of opposite types,
\[
  (a,\infty)\cap(-\infty,b)=(a,b).
\]

% REVIEW: the subbasis condition that the elements of S cover X is never checked (it fails when X is a single point, where S = {emptyset}); the inclusion "rays are open in the order topology" is also left implicit.
Thus, finite intersections of elements of $S$ give open intervals and open rays, which form a
basis for the order topology. Hence, $S$ is a subbasis and generates the order topology on $X$.
